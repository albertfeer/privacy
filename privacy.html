Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026

1. Information Collection

This application does NOT collect, read, store, upload or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII).

The application runs entirely locally on your device. It will never collect your name, phone number, geographic location, photo albums, camera, microphone, unique device ID, account data or other private information. All temporary data is only stored in local cache and will not be sent to any remote cloud server.

2. Third-Party Services

This software does not integrate any third-party tracking, advertising, analytics or data collection SDKs. No user data will be shared or forwarded to any third-party organizations.

3. Device Permissions

The app will not request sensitive device permissions automatically. If you manually grant permissions such as camera, location or storage access, all related data will only be used locally within the current session and will not be transmitted externally.

4. Contact Developer

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via email: albertstudy@163.com
